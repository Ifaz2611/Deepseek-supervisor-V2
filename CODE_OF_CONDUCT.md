# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being kind, considerate, and respectful
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

Maintainers are expected to act fairly and consistently when responding to reports.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through a private channel on GitHub. Do not create a public issue for sensitive reports. Include enough information to allow the maintainers to understand what happened, while avoiding unnecessary personal data.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter when handling a report.
